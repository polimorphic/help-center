---
title: "Dictate a message to the Assistant"
description: "Speak your question or request instead of typing it, then review the text before you send."
---

Sometimes it's faster to say what you need than to type it, especially for a long request or when you're between meetings. You can dictate a message to the Assistant, and it turns your speech into text you can review before sending.

## Before you begin

- Use a browser that allows microphone recording, such as a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. If your browser doesn't support it, the microphone button doesn't appear.
- Your computer needs a working microphone.

## Dictate a message

1. Open the Assistant and start a new chat or open an existing one.
2. Select the microphone in the message box.
3. The first time, your browser asks for permission to use your microphone. Select **Allow**.
4. Speak your message. Moving bars in the message box show it's picking up your voice.
5. Select the check mark when you're done.

After a moment, your words appear as text in the message box. Read them over, fix anything that came out wrong, and select send. Nothing is sent until you do.

If you already typed something, the dictated text is added after it.

## Cancel a dictation

- While you're speaking, select the X to stop and throw away the recording.
- While the text is being prepared, select the spinning icon to cancel.

Your message box stays as it was before you started.

## If dictation doesn't work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Microphone access was blocked. Allow microphone access in your browser to dictate.* | Allow the microphone for Polimorphic in your browser's site settings, then try again. |
| *Didn't catch that. Try speaking again.* | The recording had no speech the Assistant could pick up. Check that the right microphone is selected and speak a little closer to it. |
| *Transcription failed. Please try again.* | Try again. If it keeps happening, email support@polimorphic.com. |

## Good to know

- A single recording can run up to 10 minutes. At 10 minutes, recording stops on its own and your text is prepared.
- Dictation also works when you reply in an existing chat, not just when you start one.

**Tip:** Speak the way you'd explain the request to a coworker. You can always tidy up the text before you send it.
