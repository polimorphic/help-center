---
title: "Build a PowerPoint deck with the Assistant"
description: "Ask the Assistant to turn your information into a PowerPoint deck with slides, charts, and speaker notes."
---

A council update, a department briefing, or a quick training session usually needs slides, and building them from scratch takes time. The Assistant can turn what you've gathered in a chat into a PowerPoint (.pptx) deck you can preview, download, and finish in PowerPoint.

## Build a deck

1. Open the Assistant.
2. Gather what the deck should cover. You can ask questions first, attach files, or paste in your notes.
3. Ask for a deck and describe it, such as *Make a 6-slide deck for the council on this year's permit volume, with a chart of permits by month and speaker notes for each slide.*
4. Send your message.

The Assistant attaches the deck to its answer as a file card. It includes:

- A title slide with the deck title and your organization's name.
- One slide for each topic, with a heading and a short list of points.
- Tables and charts on their own slides when you ask for them.
- Speaker notes under each slide when you ask for them.

Decks use a plain, simple design. They don't use your organization's branding, so you may want to apply your own theme in PowerPoint before you present.

## Preview the deck

1. Select the deck's file card in the chat.
2. The deck opens in a preview next to the chat, with the current slide on top and the rest of the slides below it.
3. Select a slide below, or use the arrow keys, to move between slides.
4. Optional: select the speaker notes button to show the notes for the current slide.
5. Optional: select the expand button to view the deck full screen.

To close the preview, select the X or press **Esc**.

## Download the deck

Select the download button on the file card or in the preview. The deck downloads as a .pptx file that you can open and edit in PowerPoint.

## Change the deck

Ask for changes in the same chat, such as *Add a slide on next steps* or *Shorten the third slide.* The Assistant makes a new deck with your changes and attaches it to its new answer. The earlier deck stays in the chat.

**Tip:** Keep each slide to one idea. Slides with a lot of text don't split onto a second slide on their own, so if a slide looks crowded, ask the Assistant to break it into two.
