---
title: "Crawl your website and set a refresh cadence"
---

Your website is usually the best source of answers residents already have. Add it to the [Knowledge Base](/getting-started/glossary) and the AI reads its pages, then Polimorphic checks back on a schedule you pick so answers stay current as the site changes.

## Before you begin

- Know your website's main address, such as *https://www.fairviewtown.gov*.
- Think about which parts of the site the AI should skip, such as old news posts or a staff intranet.

## Add the website

1. Select **Knowledge Base** under **AI Front Desk** in the left menu.
2. Open the **Domains** tab.
3. Select **Add Domain**.
4. In **Website URL**, enter your website's address.
5. Wait while Polimorphic detects the site type. Check that **Site Type** is right, and change it if it isn't.
6. Select **Next**.

## Pick what to read and how often

1. Under **Availability**, pick **Everyone** or **Staff only**.
2. In **Crawl Frequency**, pick how often to check the site.
3. Optional: open **Advanced settings** and enter folders to skip in **Exclude paths**, such as */news/archive*.
4. Optional: in **Include paths**, enter folders to limit the crawl to, such as */departments*.
5. Optional: turn on **Include PDFs linked on these pages** to read PDF files the pages link to.
6. Optional: turn on **Limit crawl depth?** and set **Max crawl depth** to stop the crawl a few links away from the address you entered.
7. Select **Add Domain**.

## Which crawl frequency to pick

| Option | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| **Weekly** | Sites that post news, meeting notices, or closures often |
| **Monthly** | Most city and county sites |
| **Every 3 Months** | Sites that rarely change, such as a municipal code site |
| **No cadence (manual only)** | Sites you refresh by hand when you know they changed |

To change the schedule later, edit the domain on the **Domains** tab and select **Save Changes**.

The domain appears on the **Domains** tab, and its pages appear as sources after the first crawl. Polimorphic checks the site again on the schedule you picked. When a page changes, the next crawl updates the answers that use it.

If you change a page today and can't wait for the next crawl, open that page's source and use **Refresh this source in the AI index**.

## When a crawl reaches its page limit

Your organization has a crawl page allowance shared across its websites over a rolling 30-day period. Each crawl also has a per-run page limit.

**Stopped at the page limit** means a run ended early because it reached either limit. Pages it didn't reach aren't removed and stay searchable; they'll be refreshed on a later run.

When the organization's allowance is used up, affected crawls show **Paused** and won't run until pages free up. The notice shows when pages start to free up, if that date is available.
