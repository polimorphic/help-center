---
title: "Add documents, links, and FAQs"
---

When the AI doesn't know something residents ask about, like a new fee schedule or holiday hours, the fix is to give it a source. You can add web pages, files, and your own questions and answers to the [Knowledge Base](/getting-started/glossary), and the AI will use them in its answers.

## Before you begin

- Make sure each page or file is current. The AI repeats what it reads.
- Think about who should get answers from each source: residents and staff, or staff only.

## Add web pages

1. Select **Knowledge Base** under **AI Front Desk** in the left menu.
2. Open the **Links** tab.
3. Select **Add Link**.
4. Type a page address, such as *https://www.fairviewtown.gov/pet-registration*, and press Enter. To add several, paste them separated by commas or new lines.
5. Under **Availability**, pick **Everyone** or **Staff only**.
6. Select **Add**.

The pages show on the **Links** tab, and the AI can use them once Polimorphic finishes reading them.

## Upload documents

1. Open the **Documents** tab.
2. Select **Add Documents**.
3. Select **Choose Files** and pick one or more files, such as a PDF fee schedule, a Word handout, or a text (.txt) or Markdown (.md) file.
4. Under **Availability**, pick **Everyone** or **Staff only**.
5. Select **Add**.

The files show on the **Documents** tab, and the AI can use them once Polimorphic finishes reading them.

## Write your own FAQs

Use an [FAQ](/getting-started/glossary) (frequently asked question) when the answer isn't written clearly anywhere else, or when residents ask the same thing often.

1. Open the **FAQs** tab.
2. Select **Add FAQ**.
3. In **Question**, enter the question the way residents ask it, such as *Is the transfer station open on Saturdays?*
4. In **Answer**, enter a complete answer, such as *Yes. The transfer station is open Saturdays from 8 AM to noon, except on holidays.*
5. Under **Availability**, pick **Everyone** or **Staff only**.
6. Select **Add & Add Another** to write another FAQ, or save to finish.

Your FAQs show on the **FAQs** tab. To add many at once, select **Bulk Upload FAQs** and upload a CSV (comma-separated values) file.

## Update a source later

1. Find the source on its tab. You can search by title.
2. Select the edit icon.
3. Change **Display Name**, **Availability**, or tags with **Add tag**.
4. Turn on **Refresh this source in the AI index** if the page or file has changed.
5. Select **Save**.

Your changes are saved, and a refreshed source is read again so answers pick up the new content.

**Tip:** To stop the AI from using a source without losing it, archive it instead of deleting it.
