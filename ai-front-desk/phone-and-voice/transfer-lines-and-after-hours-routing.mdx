---
title: "Transfer lines and after-hours routing"
---

When a caller asks for a person, you want them to reach the right desk, or the right after-hours number when the office is closed. Transfer lines tell the AI where to send calls. Here's what each setting does and how the AI picks a line. Setting up for a holiday? See [Set up holiday and after-hours routing for AI Voice](/ai-front-desk/phone-and-voice/set-up-holiday-and-after-hours-routing-for-ai-voice).

Transfer lines are on the **Transfer Lines** page in **Settings**, under **General**. Phone line schedules are on the phone line itself, under **Settings**, then **Channels**, then **Voice**.

## Set up a transfer line

| Field | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | The line's name, such as *Utility Billing*. |
| **Number** | Yes | The phone number the call goes to. |
| **Extension** | No | An extension to dial after connecting. |
| **Services and Departments** | Yes | Topics this line handles, such as *water bills, shutoffs*. The AI matches caller requests to these. |
| **Confirmation Prompt** | No | What the AI asks before transferring, such as *Would you like me to transfer you to utility billing?* |

## How the AI decides where a call goes

1. When a caller asks for a person, the AI looks for a transfer line that matches the request.
2. If none matches, the call goes to the phone line's **Default Transfer Line**.
3. If the matched line is outside its hours and has an alternate number, the call goes to the alternate number.
4. If the line is outside its hours and has no alternate number, the AI treats the line as closed. It doesn't dial the main number.

## After-hours and holiday routing

Transfer lines can send calls to an alternate number outside their hours, and the phone line can switch greetings and default transfer lines on a schedule or for a holiday. See [Set up holiday and after-hours routing for AI Voice](/ai-front-desk/phone-and-voice/set-up-holiday-and-after-hours-routing-for-ai-voice) for how to set that up.

## Add many transfer lines at once

To add many transfer lines at once, select **Bulk Upload**, then **Download CSV Template**. Fill in the template and select **Upload CSV File**.

The new lines appear on the **Transfer Lines** tab.

## See transfers in analytics

The **Voice Interactions by Transfer Line** chart labels calls sent to the phone line's default number as *Default*. Calls to numbers that match no transfer line are labeled *Unmatched*.
