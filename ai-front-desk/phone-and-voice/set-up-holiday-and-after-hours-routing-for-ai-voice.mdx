---
title: "Set up holiday and after-hours routing for AI Voice"
---

Holidays are when residents are most likely to call an office that's closed. A few minutes of setup ahead of time means the AI greets them with the right message and sends anyone who needs a person to a number that's actually staffed. This article covers holiday schedules, regular after-hours schedules, and what the AI does when no one is available.

Phone line schedules are on the phone line itself, under **Settings**, then **Channels**, then **Voice**. Transfer lines are on the **Transfer Lines** page in **Settings**, under **General**.

## How the pieces fit together

Three settings decide what a caller hears and where they're sent:

- **The phone line's defaults.** The default greeting message and **Default Transfer Line** you set when you created the phone line. These apply whenever no schedule does.
- **Phone line schedules.** On the phone line's **Schedule** step, **Time-Based** and **Holiday-Based** configurations swap in a different greeting and default transfer line at set times.
- **Transfer line hours.** Each transfer line can have its own hours and an alternate number to use outside them.

On a day that has both a holiday and a time-based schedule, the holiday wins.

## Set up a holiday

1. Open the phone line and go to the **Schedule** step.
2. Select the **Holiday-Based** tab, then **Add Holiday**.
3. Enter a **Holiday Name**, such as *Thanksgiving*.
4. Pick the dates on the calendar. You can pick more than one, such as Thanksgiving and the day after.
5. Write a **Greeting Message** that says the office is closed and when it reopens. For example: *"Town Hall is closed for Thanksgiving and reopens Monday at 8 AM. I can still help with most questions."*
6. Enter a **Default Transfer Line** for callers who need a person, such as your on-call or emergency line.
7. Leave **Apply to all transfer lines** checked. That way the AI knows about the holiday and its transfer number whenever it looks at any of your transfer lines, not just the phone line's default.
8. Select **Update Phone Line** (or **Save Phone Line** for a new line).

On the dates you picked, callers hear the holiday greeting all day, and the AI uses the holiday's transfer number as its default.

Holiday dates are specific calendar dates, so they don't repeat on their own. Add next year's dates when you plan for the next holiday season. The calendar on the **Holiday-Based** tab shows every holiday date you've already added.

## Set up weeknight and weekend hours

For closures that repeat every week, use a time-based schedule instead.

1. On the phone line's **Schedule** step, select the **Time-Based** tab, then **Add Configuration**.
2. Fill in **Configuration Name**, **Days of Week**, **Start Time**, **End Time**, and **Timezone**.
3. Write a **Greeting Message** and choose a **Default Transfer Line** for those hours.
4. Save the phone line.

For example, weeknights 5 PM to 8 AM: *"Town Hall is closed. I can still help with most questions."* A start time later than the end time runs overnight.

When two time-based schedules overlap, the one higher in the list applies. Use the arrows to change the order.

## Send a transfer line to an after-hours number

A time-based configuration on a transfer line sets the line's hours and the number to use outside them. Open the transfer line and select **Add Configuration**.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Days of Week** | The days the configuration applies. |
| **Start Time** and **End Time** | The hours it applies. |
| **Timezone** | The time zone for those hours. |
| **Alternate Phone Number** | The number used outside the line's hours, such as an after-hours answering service. |
| **Name of Alternate Number** | A name for that number. |
| **Transfer Confirmation Prompt for Alternate Number** | What the AI asks before sending a call to the alternate number. |

A transfer line's hours are set by day of the week and time, so they don't change on a holiday. A line open on weekdays still counts as open on a weekday holiday. To cover holidays, set the holiday's **Default Transfer Line** and leave **Apply to all transfer lines** checked.

## What happens when no one is available

1. When a caller asks for a person, the AI looks for a transfer line that matches the request.
2. If none matches, the AI offers the default transfer line for right now. On a holiday, that's the holiday's **Default Transfer Line**.
3. If the matched line is outside its hours and has an alternate number, the AI offers the alternate number.
4. If the line is outside its hours and has no alternate number, the AI tells the caller that line isn't available right now. It doesn't dial the main number.

If you leave a holiday's **Default Transfer Line** blank, the AI has no default number to offer that day. It doesn't fall back to the phone line's regular default.

If the phone line has no default transfer line and no voicemail intake, you'll see a **No Transfer Fallback** warning when you save. To give callers somewhere to go, select **Create Voicemail Intake** on the phone line's first step so they can leave a message.

## Before the holiday

- The holiday is added with this year's dates.
- The greeting says the office is closed and when it reopens.
- The holiday's **Default Transfer Line** goes to a number someone will answer.
- **Apply to all transfer lines** is checked.
- Transfer lines that need after-hours coverage have an alternate number.

**Tip:** to hear the holiday greeting ahead of time, add today's date to the holiday, call the line, then remove today's date and save again.
