---
title: "Review interaction logs"
---

The best way to know how the AI is really doing is to read what residents asked and what it told them. [Interaction logs](/getting-started/glossary) show every conversation, the answer the AI gave, and the sources it used.

## Before you begin

- Personal details such as names and phone numbers are hidden in logs by default. See [Reveal redacted personal information in a log](/ai-front-desk/monitoring/reveal-redacted-personal-information-in-a-log).
- Your organization's data retention setting controls how long conversation text is kept.

## Open the log

1. Select **Log** under **AI Front Desk** in the left menu.

The **Interaction Logs** page lists conversations, newest first.

## Find the conversations you care about

1. Set **Date** to the time period you'd like to see.
2. Optional: in **Interaction Type**, pick a channel, such as **Chat**, **Search**, **Voice**, or **SMS**.
3. Optional: in **Outcome**, pick **Handled** or **Not Handled**.
4. Optional: in **Sentiment**, pick **Positive** or **Negative**.
5. Optional: filter by **Category**, **Language**, or **Transfer Line**.
6. Optional: in **Tool Used**, pick a capability, such as **GIS Lookup**, to see conversations where the AI used it.
7. Optional: in **Log Type**, pick **Live logs** for real residents or **Test logs** for staff testing.

The list updates to show only matching conversations.

## Read a conversation

1. Select a conversation in the list.
2. Read the **AI Summary** for a short version.
3. Read the full conversation. If the resident wrote in another language, switch between **Original** and **English**.
4. Check the sources listed with each answer to see where the AI got its information.

## Follow up on a conversation

- Select **Create a Case** to start a [case](/getting-started/glossary) from the conversation. If a case already exists, select **Go to Case**.
- Select **Add to Health Check** to add the question to your test set.
- Select **Download / Share** or **Copy Link** to share the conversation with a coworker.
- If the resident's name appears, select it to open their [contact](/getting-started/glossary) record.

**Tip:** A quick weekly look at **Not Handled** and **Negative** conversations is the easiest way to spot where answers fall short.
