---
title: "How to export your AI analytics data"
---

When leadership asks how the AI is doing, or you want to dig into the numbers outside Polimorphic, you can export your AI analytics as a report.

## Export a report

1. Go to **Analytics** in the left-hand sidebar and open the AI analytics view.
2. Use the date range and other filters at the top of the page to pick the period you'd like to report on.
3. Click the **Create Report** (download) icon at the top right of the filters.

A confirmation lets you know the export has started. Polimorphic builds the report in the background and emails you when it's ready.

## Good to know

- The report uses whatever filters were selected when you clicked **Create Report**.
- Large date ranges can take a few minutes. You don't need to stay on the page.
- If the export fails, you'll see an error message. Try again, or contact support if it keeps happening.
