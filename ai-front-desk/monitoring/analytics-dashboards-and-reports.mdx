---
title: "Analytics dashboards and reports"
---

When your manager asks how the AI is doing, or you're planning staffing for a busy season, analytics has the numbers. Here's where each dashboard lives and what every chart shows.

## Find your dashboards

| Page | How to open it | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Analytics** | Select **Analytics** near the top of the left menu | Dashboards for **Cases**, **AI**, and **Team**, plus any dashboards set up for your organization |
| **Front Desk Analytics** | Select **Analytics** under **AI Front Desk** | AI chat, search, text, and phone activity |

On the **Analytics** page, **Showing dashboards for** tells you which organization you're viewing. Staff can see different dashboards depending on their access.

## Narrow the AI analytics page

- **Date range**: **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Last 30 days**, or **Custom Range**.
- **Interaction type**: one channel, or **All** for every channel combined. The weekly AI digest email uses **All**.

## Read the AI metrics

| Metric or chart | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Handle Rate** | The share of interactions the AI handled without staff. |
| **Time Saved** | An estimate of staff time the AI saved. |
| **Average Quality** | The average quality score of AI answers. |
| **User Satisfaction Rate** | How residents rated answers. |
| **Interactions over Time** | Interaction count by day. |
| **Interactions by Category** | The top 10 topics by interaction count. Everything else is grouped into Other. |
| **Volume by Hour of the Day** | When residents reach out. Useful for staffing. |
| **Session Analytics** | **Engaged** and **Abandoned** sessions. |
| **Call Distribution** | Calls **Transferred** and **Not transferred**. |
| **Voice Interactions by Transfer Line** | Where transferred calls went. *Default* means the phone line's default number. *Unmatched* means no matching line. |
| **Performance over Time** | How the main metrics change over the period. |

## Download a report

Select **Create Report** on **Front Desk Analytics** to download a PDF. The PDF uses the same charts and data you see on screen, including total engaged interactions. The monthly interactions chart labels each channel's count, even when the count is small.

The PDF downloads to your computer, ready to share. Reports can't include dates before January 1, 2026.

## Good to know

- Charts briefly show a loading indicator after you change a filter.
- If your organization has no engaged AI interactions in a period, no AI digest email is sent for it.
