---
title: "Reveal redacted personal information in a log"
---

Sometimes you need to follow up with a resident after an AI conversation, like returning a call the AI promised. Polimorphic hides personal details in logs by default, but you can reveal them when your work requires it.

PII (personally identifiable information) is anything that can identify a person, such as a name, phone number, email address, or street address. Hiding it means staff reviewing AI answers don't see residents' personal details unless they need to.

## Before you begin

- Revealing details takes access to [interaction log](/getting-started/glossary) personal information. The *Knowledge Base Admin (PII)* role template includes it.
- Have a work reason ready. You'll pick one each time.

## Reveal the details

1. Select **Log** under **AI Front Desk** in the left menu.
2. Open the conversation.
3. Select **Reveal Redactions**. This button appears only when the conversation has hidden details.
4. In **Reason for Access**, pick the reason that fits: **Customer Support**, **Legal or Compliance**, **Audit Review**, **Engineering Debug**, or **Other**.
5. Select **Reveal PII**.

The conversation shows the resident's details in place of the hidden text. Polimorphic records each reveal with your name, the conversation, and the reason you picked.

## Hide the details again

1. Select **Restore Redactions**.

The details are hidden again.

**Tip:** Hide the details as soon as you have what you need, especially if your screen is visible to others.

Example: a resident calls the clerk's office to say the AI promised a callback but no one called. The clerk opens the conversation, reveals the details with the reason **Customer Support**, finds the phone number, and returns the call.
