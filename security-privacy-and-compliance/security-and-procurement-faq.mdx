title: "Security and procurement FAQ"
---

When your IT, security, or procurement team is reviewing Polimorphic, these are the questions they usually ask first. For anything not covered here, your Polimorphic representative is happy to help.

## Security and compliance

### Is Polimorphic SOC 2 compliant?

Yes. For the audit report, visit the Polimorphic Trust Center at [trust.polimorphic.com](https://trust.polimorphic.com/).

### Is data encrypted?

Yes. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

### Does Polimorphic support multi-factor authentication?

Yes. Polimorphic supports multi-factor authentication (MFA). Organizations can also require single sign-on.

### Can Polimorphic support HIPAA requirements?

Polimorphic can support HIPAA-compliant setups with a business associate agreement (BAA) when protected health information is involved. Talk to your Polimorphic contact and they'll help you get started.

### Where can I get security documents?

Visit the Polimorphic Trust Center at [trust.polimorphic.com](https://trust.polimorphic.com/). For a summary of what's there, see [Polimorphic Trust Center](/security-privacy-and-compliance/polimorphic-trust-center). For a security questionnaire, or a question the Trust Center doesn't answer, contact your Polimorphic representative.

## Access and identity

### Does Polimorphic support single sign-on?

Yes. Polimorphic supports single sign-on ([SSO](/getting-started/glossary)) with SAML, including Microsoft Entra ID.

### Can we control what each staff member can do?

Yes. Access comes from groups and roles in each organization, so each person only sees what their role allows.

## Data

### Can we control how long conversation data is kept?

Yes. Administrators pick how long AI transcripts are kept for each channel.

### How is personal information in AI conversations protected?

It's hidden in logs by default. Only permitted staff can reveal it, and every reveal is recorded with a reason.

## Accessibility

### Do you have a VPAT?

Yes. Polimorphic provides a VPAT in the Revised Section 508 edition. Ask your Polimorphic contact for the current version.

## Integrations

### Which systems can Polimorphic connect to?

Ready-made integrations include Cityworks, Tyler Munis ARWS, Cayenta Utility Billing, FastTrack, Catalis Request311, and SharePoint. You can also build a custom integration for other systems.
