---
title: "Data retention and interaction log expiry"
---

Your state's records rules, or your own policies, may say how long you should keep resident conversations. The **Data Retention** settings let you decide how long Polimorphic keeps AI conversation transcripts for each channel.

## Open the Data Retention settings

Open **Settings**, then select **Data Retention** under **Security**.

## What each setting covers

| Section | Setting | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transcripts | **Chat transcripts** | Resident and staff messages in the chat widget. |
| Transcripts | **Search transcripts** | Resident searches and AI answers. |
| Transcripts | **Voice transcripts** | Call recordings and transcripts from the AI phone line. |
| Transcripts | **SMS & WhatsApp transcripts** | Text message and WhatsApp conversations. |
| Transcripts | **Email transcripts** | Email conversations handled by the AI. |
| Transcripts | **AI assistant logs** | Prompts, responses, and steps from the staff assistant. |

## Pick how long to keep each one

| Choice | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| A number with **Days**, **Months**, or **Years** | Conversations older than this are removed. |
| **Forever** | Conversations are kept. This is the default. |

Select **Save** to apply your changes, or **Reset** to undo changes you haven't saved. Once you save, the new limits apply to that channel's conversations.

## What happens when a conversation expires

- The words of the conversation are removed: questions, answers, and search terms.
- Basic technical details stay, such as when it happened and how long it took, so your analytics counts stay accurate.
- Removed conversations no longer appear in [interaction logs](/getting-started/glossary).
- Removal can't be undone.

## Before you shorten a limit

- Check your state's records retention schedule. Your clerk or records officer can tell you what you have to keep.
- Export anything you need before the change takes effect.
- It's a good idea to let your team know before you shorten a limit, since older conversations will disappear from their logs.
