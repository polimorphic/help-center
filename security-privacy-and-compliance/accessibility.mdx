---
title: "Accessibility"
---

Polimorphic serves residents of all abilities, so we work to make the resident experience and staff tools usable with a keyboard, with screen readers, and with readable color contrast.

## Get our accessibility documents

Polimorphic provides a Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT), also called an Accessibility Conformance Report, in the Revised Section 508 edition. Section 508 is the U.S. federal accessibility standard. Ask your Polimorphic contact for the current version.

## Keep your own content accessible

What you add affects accessibility too. When you customize the chat widget or build forms:

- Pick colors that pass the contrast check.
- Write clear question labels, such as "Property address" instead of "Address 2."
- Upload documents that screen readers can read, such as tagged PDFs.

## Report an accessibility problem

If you or a resident has trouble using Polimorphic with assistive technology, contact Polimorphic support. Let us know the page, the device and browser, and the assistive technology used, and we'll look into it.
