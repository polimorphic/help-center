---

When your IT, security, legal, or procurement team starts asking how Polimorphic protects your data, point them to the Polimorphic Trust Center at [trust.polimorphic.com](https://trust.polimorphic.com/). It's where Polimorphic's security, privacy, and compliance information lives, and it's open to anyone.

## What you'll find there

- **Compliance:** SOC 2, HIPAA, WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and CSA STAR.
- **Controls:** the security controls Polimorphic maintains, grouped into infrastructure security, organizational security, product security, internal security procedures, and data and privacy. Examples include data encryption, restricted production access, penetration testing, and deleting customer data when a customer leaves. The controls are monitored continuously through Vanta, and the page shows when they were last checked.
- **Subprocessors:** the third-party services Polimorphic uses to run the platform, such as Amazon Web Services for cloud hosting. Select **View all** on the Trust Center for the full list.
- **Privacy policy:** a link to Polimorphic's privacy policy.

## Find what your team needs

1. Go to [trust.polimorphic.com](https://trust.polimorphic.com/).
2. Use the **Overview**, **Controls**, and **Subprocessors** tabs to find what you need.

**Tip:** Sending the link before a security review starts can save a round of questions.

## Need something else?

For the SOC 2 report or other security documents, a security questionnaire, a business associate agreement (BAA), or a question the Trust Center doesn't answer, contact your Customer Success Manager or [support@polimorphic.com](mailto:support@polimorphic.com).
