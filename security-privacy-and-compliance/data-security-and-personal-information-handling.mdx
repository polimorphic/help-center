title: "Data security and personal information handling"
---

Residents share names, addresses, phone numbers, and sometimes sensitive details with your organization. When your IT team, an auditor, or a resident asks how that information is protected and who can see it, this page has the answers.

Polimorphic protects your data with its own security controls, plus access and retention settings that you manage.

### How Polimorphic protects your data

- Polimorphic is SOC 2 compliant. SOC 2 is an independent audit of security controls.
- Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Polimorphic supports multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Polimorphic can support HIPAA-compliant setups with a business associate agreement (BAA) when protected health information is involved. HIPAA is the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.

For security documents and audit reports, visit the Polimorphic Trust Center at [trust.polimorphic.com](https://trust.polimorphic.com/).

### Who can see what

Access comes from groups and roles in each organization, so staff only see the features and data their roles allow. Your administrator manages this. See [roles and permissions](/administration/roles-and-permissions).

With single sign-on ([SSO](/getting-started/glossary)), your IT team controls who can sign in and which groups they join. See [setting up SSO](/administration/set-up-single-sign-on-saml-or-microsoft-entra-id).

### Personal information in AI conversations

Personal information in AI conversation logs is hidden by default. Only staff with a special permission can reveal it. They have to pick a reason each time, and every reveal is recorded. See [revealing redacted information](/ai-front-desk/monitoring/reveal-redacted-personal-information-in-a-log).

### How long data is kept

Your organization chooses how long AI conversation transcripts are kept. See [data retention](/security-privacy-and-compliance/data-retention-and-interaction-log-expiry).

## Example: a records audit

An auditor asks the City of Fairview who viewed a caller's phone number. The city's administrator confirms that only two staff members have the permission to reveal personal information in logs, and that each reveal is recorded with the person, the conversation, and the reason they chose.
