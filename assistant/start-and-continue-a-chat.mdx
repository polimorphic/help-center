---
title: "Start and continue a chat with the Assistant"
description: "Ask the Assistant a question, attach files, and pick up earlier chats from your history."
---

When a question comes up mid-task, you can ask the Assistant without leaving what you're doing for long. Each conversation is saved as a chat, so you can come back later, keep going, or find something it told you last week.

## Start a chat

1. Select **Assistant** at the top of any page. You can also press **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on a Mac) and pick **Open Assistant**.
2. Optional: pick a suggestion below the message box, such as **Look up a resident** or **Ask about cases**, to point the Assistant at that kind of information.
3. Type your question in the message box, such as *Which open cases are assigned to me and due this week?*
4. Press **Enter** or select the send button. To add a new line without sending, press **Shift+Enter**.

The Assistant shows the steps it's working through, such as searching your knowledge base, and then writes its answer. Links to the contacts, cases, and pages it used appear under the answer. The chat gets a title and shows up in your chat history on the left.

If you work in more than one organization, pick the right one from the menu in the message box before you send your first message. The Assistant answers from that organization's information.

## Attach files to your question

You can ask the Assistant about a file, such as *Summarize the attached inspection report* or *Which rows in this spreadsheet are missing an address?*

1. Select the paperclip in the message box, or drag files onto the message box.
2. Wait for each file to finish uploading. It appears as a card above your message.
3. Type your question and send it.

You can attach PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), text, Markdown, CSV, and HTML files, plus PNG, JPG, and WebP images. Each file can be up to 25 MB. To remove a file before you send, select the X on its card.

## Keep a conversation going

Reply in the same chat to ask a follow-up, such as *Now just the ones in Ward 3*. The Assistant uses what's already in the conversation, so you don't need to repeat yourself.

If you leave a chat while the Assistant is still answering, it keeps working. Open the chat again and the answer picks up where it left off.

## Find an earlier chat

1. Open the Assistant.
2. Find the chat under **Chats** in the history on the left, or select **Search for a chat** and type a word from it.
3. Select the chat.

The full conversation opens, and you can keep replying.

**Tip:** The history panel collapses so you have more room. Select the panel button at the top of the history to open or close it.

## Rename or delete a chat

1. Select the arrow next to the chat's title at the top of the conversation, or hover over the chat in your history and select the actions menu.
2. Pick **Rename** or **Delete**.
3. Confirm your choice.

A renamed chat shows its new title in your history. A deleted chat is removed from your history and can't be restored.

## Start fresh

Select **New chat** at the top of the history. A new conversation is the best choice when you switch topics, since the Assistant won't mix in details from the last one.

## Good to know

- If your organization has set AI usage policies, some requests may be blocked. You'll see *Your AI usage policy doesn't allow this request* in the message box. Rephrase your request, or check with your administrator.
- If the Assistant answers from a connected SharePoint site and your access has expired, it asks you to reconnect right in the chat.
