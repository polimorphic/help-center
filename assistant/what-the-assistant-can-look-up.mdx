---
title: "What the Assistant can look up for you"
description: "See the kinds of information the Assistant can find, with example questions for each."
---

Knowing what the Assistant can reach makes it easier to ask the right question. You don't need to tell it where to look. Ask the way you'd ask a coworker, and it picks the right place to search, sometimes more than one.

What it can look up follows your access. If your role doesn't let you see something in Polimorphic, the Assistant can't find it for you either. See [Roles and permissions](/administration/roles-and-permissions).

## Answer questions from your Knowledge Base

The Assistant searches your organization's [Knowledge Base](/getting-started/glossary): your website pages, documents, FAQs, and any sources marked as staff only. When the first results don't have the full answer, it checks related pages and reads further into long pages to find details like fees or deadlines.

Try asking:

- *What's the fee for a residential fence permit?*
- *What pages do we have about trash pickup?*
- *Summarize our snow removal policy.*

The pages it used appear as sources under the answer, so you can check them. If an answer is wrong or out of date, fix the source. See [Improve a wrong or weak answer](/ai-front-desk/how-the-ai-answers/improve-a-wrong-or-weak-answer).

## Look up a resident

The Assistant searches your contacts by name, email, phone number, or address. Matching contacts appear under the answer, and you can select one to open it.

Try asking:

- *What's Maria Lopez's phone number?*
- *Who is jsmith@example.com?*
- *Which contact lives at 120 Oak Street?*

## Find cases

The Assistant finds [cases](/getting-started/glossary) by case ID, status, Workflow, requester, assignee, case manager, tag, or date. Matching cases appear under the answer, and you can select one to open it.

Try asking:

- *Which open cases are assigned to me?*
- *Show cases submitted by Jane Doe this month.*
- *What's the status of case 4f2k9b1c?*

## Check address-based information

If your organization has added map layers to Polimorphic, such as trash routes or voting districts, the Assistant can look up what applies to a specific address.

Try asking:

- *What day is trash pickup at 45 Elm Avenue?*
- *Which council district is 300 Main Street in?*

## Get information from connected systems

If your organization has connected other systems to Polimorphic and made them available to the Assistant, it can look up information there too, such as utility billing or work orders. What's available depends on your organization's integrations. See [Get started with integrations](/integrations/get-started-with-integrations).

## Work with files you attach

The Assistant reads PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, slide decks, text files, and images you attach to a message. See [Start and continue a chat with the Assistant](/assistant/start-and-continue-a-chat#attach-files-to-your-question).

## Create charts, documents, and decks

The Assistant can turn what it finds into a chart or table in the chat, or a Word, PDF, Excel, or PowerPoint file. See [Create charts, tables, and documents in the Assistant](/assistant/create-charts-tables-and-documents) and [Build a PowerPoint deck with the Assistant](/assistant/build-a-powerpoint-deck).

## If it can't find something

- Check that you picked the right organization in the message box.
- Try naming what you're looking for more specifically, such as a full name or case ID.
- If you think you should have access, see [I don't see a feature or button](/troubleshooting/troubleshooting/i-don-t-see-a-feature-or-button).
