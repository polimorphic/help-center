title: "What is the Assistant?"
description: "Meet the AI Assistant for staff: what it can help with, who can use it, and what it can see."
---

Some questions come up all day: what's the fee for a fence permit, which cases are still open for a resident, what did the council's latest policy say. The Assistant is a private AI chat for your staff that answers those questions from your organization's information, so you don't have to dig through folders or switch between screens.

The Assistant is for staff. It's separate from the [AI Front Desk](/getting-started/glossary), which answers residents on your website, by text, and by phone.

## What you can ask it to do

- **Answer questions about your organization.** It searches your [knowledge base](/getting-started/glossary), including sources marked as staff only that residents never see.
- **Look up a resident.** It searches your contacts by name, email, phone number, or address.
- **Ask about cases.** It finds [cases](/getting-started/glossary) by case ID, status, Workflow, requester, assignee, tag, or date, and links to each one.
- **Read files you attach.** Drop in a PDF, Word document, spreadsheet, slide deck, or image, and ask questions about it.
- **Draft content.** Ask for an email, a letter, a summary, or talking points, and edit the draft in the chat.
- **Create files.** It can make charts and tables in the chat, and Word, PDF, Excel, and PowerPoint files you can download. See [Create charts, tables, and documents in the Assistant](/assistant/create-charts-tables-and-documents) and [Build a PowerPoint deck with the Assistant](/assistant/build-a-powerpoint-deck).

For more examples of what it can find, see [What the Assistant can look up for you](/assistant/what-the-assistant-can-look-up).

## Who can use it

Every staff member who signs in to Polimorphic can open the Assistant. What it can look up for you follows your access:

- It only searches contacts if your role lets you view contacts.
- It only searches cases if your role lets you view cases.
- It only answers from the knowledge base if your role lets you view the knowledge base.

So two coworkers can ask the same question and get different answers if their access is different. If the Assistant says it can't find something you expected, check [I don't see a feature or button](/troubleshooting/troubleshooting/i-don-t-see-a-feature-or-button) and [Roles and permissions](/administration/roles-and-permissions).

## Who can see your chats

Your chats are yours. Coworkers don't see them in their Assistant. Administrators can review Assistant conversations for compliance, which is noted under the message box in every chat.

To review them, administrators select **Settings** in the left menu, then **Assistant Logs** under **Security**. The list works like the AI Front Desk log. See [Review interaction logs](/ai-front-desk/monitoring/review-interaction-logs).

How long chats are kept depends on your organization's data retention setting. See [Data retention and interaction log expiry](/security-privacy-and-compliance/data-retention-and-interaction-log-expiry).

**Tip:** Treat the Assistant like a work email. Keep it to work topics, and double-check anything you'll send to a resident.
