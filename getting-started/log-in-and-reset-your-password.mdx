---
title: "Log in and reset your password"
---

Whether it's your first day or you've just forgotten your password, here's how to get into Polimorphic. You'll sign in with your organization's single sign-on ([SSO](/getting-started/glossary)) or with a password.

## Before you begin

- Look for the invitation email from your administrator. It sets up your Polimorphic staff account.
- Check with your administrator or IT team whether your organization uses SSO. SSO lets you sign in with your work account, such as Microsoft. If your organization uses SSO, you don't need a separate Polimorphic password.

## Sign in with single sign-on

1. Go to the Polimorphic sign-in page.
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Select **Continue**.
4. If Polimorphic recognizes your organization's SSO, select **Continue to** followed by your organization's name. If not, select **Sign in with Microsoft** or **Sign in with Okta**, whichever your organization uses.
5. Complete the sign-in on your organization's sign-in screen.

You're signed in and land in Polimorphic.

## Sign in with a password

1. Go to the Polimorphic sign-in page.
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Select **Continue**.
4. Enter your password.
5. If you're asked for a **Verification Code**, check your email and enter the 6-digit code. Select **Send a new code** if it doesn't arrive.
6. Select **Sign in**.

You're signed in. If you'd rather skip the password, select **Sign in without a password** and Polimorphic emails you a sign-in link.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, enter your work email address.
2. Select **Forgot password?**
3. Select **Reset Password**.
4. Open the reset email from Polimorphic and select the link.
5. Enter a new password in **New Password**.
6. Enter it again in **Confirm Password**.
7. Submit the form.

Your new password has to meet your organization's rules. The page shows a checklist, such as a minimum length or a required number, and every password needs at least 8 characters.

Once it's accepted, you'll see "Password successfully reset" and go back to the sign-in page, where you can sign in with your new password.

## Good to know

- If your organization uses SSO, reset your password through your organization's IT team, not in Polimorphic.
- Reset links expire. If a link doesn't work, start the reset again to get a new one.
- If the reset email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder. Also make sure you used the same email address your invitation was sent to.
- To change a password you know, open your account menu in the top-right corner, go to **Account Settings**, then **Password**.
