---
title: "Quickstart: your first day"
---

Welcome to Polimorphic. Your first day goes smoother when you've walked through the basics once, so this tutorial follows a real situation from start to finish: a resident calls to report a missed trash pickup. You'll sign in, find your work, log the call as a [case](/getting-started/glossary), loop in a coworker, and close the case. It takes about 15 minutes.

## Before you begin

- Look for the invitation email from your administrator. It sets up your Polimorphic staff account.

## Step 1: Sign in

1. Open the sign-in page your administrator gave you.
2. Enter your work email address and select **Continue**.
3. Sign in the way your organization does: enter your password and select **Sign in**, or use the single sign-on ([SSO](/getting-started/glossary)) button, such as **Sign in with Microsoft**.

If you get stuck, see [Log in and reset your password](/getting-started/log-in-and-reset-your-password).

## Step 2: Look around

The left sidebar holds the main areas. You'll use two of them today:

- **Cases**: every request you can see.
- **Contacts**: the people and organizations your team works with.

At the top of the page is a search bar that says **Search, ask a question, or quick create**. You can also open it with Ctrl+K (Windows) or Command+K (Mac).

## Step 3: Find your work

1. Select **Cases** in the left sidebar.
2. Select the **My To-Do** tab at the top of the table. This tab shows cases that need something from you.
3. Look for the **Your Turn** label in the **Assignees** column. It marks cases where you're assigned to the current step.
4. Select the **All Cases** tab to see every case you have access to.

On your first day, **My To-Do** may be empty. That's normal.

## Step 4: Log a phone call as a new case

A resident, Daniel Kim, calls to say his trash wasn't picked up at 120 Oak Street. You'll log the call as a case so the right team can follow up.

1. Select the search bar at the top of the page.
2. Select **Open manual case**.
3. In **Case Requester**, search for Daniel Kim and select him. If he isn't listed, leave the field blank.
4. In **Case Subject**, enter *Missed trash pickup, 120 Oak Street*.
5. Under **Log Interaction**, select **Call**.
6. Type a short summary of the call, such as *Resident reports Tuesday pickup was missed. Bins were at the curb by 6 a.m.*
7. Under **Case Location**, add 120 Oak Street.
8. Select **Start Case**.

The new case opens. The call you logged appears on the case's **Activity** tab.

## Step 5: Leave a note for a coworker

Notes are a quick way to loop in a teammate. They're for staff only, and residents never see them.

1. On the case, select the **Activity** tab.
2. Select **Leave a note or add attachment**.
3. Type *@* and the name of a coworker on the sanitation team, then select their name.
4. Type *Can you add 120 Oak St to tomorrow's makeup route?*
5. Select **Add Note**.

Your coworker gets an email with a link to the case.

## Step 6: Close the case

Once the sanitation team confirms the pickup, you can close the case.

1. At the top of the case, select the status, which shows **Open**.
2. Select **Closed**.

The case leaves your active views but stays in your records and reports.

## What you learned

- How to sign in and find the main areas.
- How to find cases that need you on the **My To-Do** tab.
- How to turn a phone call into a case.
- How to bring in a coworker with an internal note.
- How to close a case when the work is done.
