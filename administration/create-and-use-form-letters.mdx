---
title: "Create and use form letters"
description: "Save branded email templates your team can start from when writing to residents."
---

Your team probably sends some of the same emails over and over, like a permit approval or a request for missing documents. A [form letter](/getting-started/glossary) is a saved email template, so everyone starts from the same approved wording and branding.

## Create a form letter

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu, then select **Form Letters** under **Customization**.
2. Select **Create Form Letter**.
3. Enter a **Name** your team will recognize, such as *Permit approved*.
4. Turn on **Use organizational branding** if you want the letter to use your organization's branding.
5. Write the letter in the **Form Letter Editor**. You can add a **Description** so coworkers know when to use it.
6. Select **Preview** to check how it looks, then save.

The form letter appears in the **Form Letters** list, and your team can use it right away.

## Use a form letter in an email

1. Open the case and start an email to the resident.
2. Select **Apply Form Letter** and pick the letter.
3. Fill in any details for this resident, then send.

See [Communicate with a resident from a case](/working-in-polimorphic/cases/communicate-with-a-resident-from-a-case).

## Edit or delete a form letter

1. Open **Form Letters** under **Customization**. You can use **Search form letters** to find one quickly.
2. Select the letter to open it in the editor.
3. Make your changes and save, or select **Delete** to remove it.

Changes apply the next time someone uses the letter. Emails already sent don't change.

## Good to know

- In the editor, you may see a box that says *AI reply goes here*. That's where an AI-written reply is placed when the letter is used for an AI email. It's ignored when someone applies the letter to an email they write themselves.
