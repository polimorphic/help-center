---
title: "Invite staff and manage accounts"
---

When someone joins your team, you'll want them in Polimorphic with the right group from day one. When someone leaves, you can remove their access just as quickly.

## Before you begin

- Have each person's work email address handy.
- Know which group each person belongs in, such as *Building Department*. Groups decide what people can see and do. See [Roles and permissions](/administration/roles-and-permissions).
- If your organization uses single sign-on ([SSO](/getting-started/glossary)), group membership may come from your identity provider instead. See [Set up single sign-on](/administration/set-up-single-sign-on-saml-or-microsoft-entra-id).

## Invite new staff

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu, then **Access**, then **Users**.
2. Select **Add User**.
3. In **User emails**, type or paste one or more email addresses.
4. In **Group**, pick the group they should join.
5. Leave **Send invite emails** on so each person gets an invitation.
6. Confirm to add them.

They show up on the **Users** view with their group, and each person gets an invitation email. The link in it works for three days.

## What your new teammate does next

1. Opens the invitation and sets a password in **New Password** and **Confirm Password**.
2. Enters their **First Name** and **Last Name**.
3. Picks their notification settings.
4. Accepts the terms and selects **Finish Set Up**.

Once they finish, they can sign in. If your organization uses SSO, they sign in with their work account instead of setting a password.

## Move someone to a different group

1. On the **Users** view, select the person.
2. Under **Groups**, add or remove groups.
3. Save your changes.

What they can see and do now follows their new groups. A lock icon means SSO manages that group, so you can't change its members here. Make the change in your identity provider instead.

## Remove someone who's leaving

1. On the **Users** view, select the person.
2. Select **Remove Member**.
3. Confirm.

They lose access to your organization.

**Tip:** Before you remove someone, reassign any open [cases](/getting-started/glossary) or tasks they own so nothing stalls.
