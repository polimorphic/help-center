---
title: "Organization settings and tags"
---

As your team settles into Polimorphic, you'll want a shared way to sort [cases](/getting-started/glossary) and [contacts](/getting-started/glossary), and a place to manage your organization's details. That all lives in organization settings.

**Tip:** Agree on your tags with your team before you add them. Short, clear names, such as *Senior resident* or *Ward 3*, keep everyone tagging the same way.

## Open organization settings

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu.

The settings page groups related settings. Common ones include:

- **General**: your organization's details and sub-organizations.
- **Access**: users, groups, and roles.
- **SSO & Identity**: single sign-on.
- **Email Domains**: domains you send email from.
- **Tags**: labels for cases and contacts.

## Add a tag

1. In **Settings**, select **Tags**.
2. Select **Add Tag**.
3. In **Name**, enter the tag name, such as *Ward 3*.
4. In **Color**, pick a color.
5. Optional: in **Description**, explain when staff should use it.
6. Save the tag.

The tag is ready to use on cases and contacts, and as a recipient filter for [outbound communications](/getting-started/glossary).

## Edit a tag

1. Find the tag in the list.
2. Select **Edit tag**.
3. Change the name, color, or description.
4. Save your changes.

The change shows everywhere the tag is used.

## Delete a tag

1. Find the tag in the list.
2. Select **Delete tag**.
3. Confirm.

The tag is removed from every contact and case that has it. You can't undo this. If an outbound communication targets that tag, update it before you delete the tag.

## Update your organization's details

1. In **Settings**, select **General**.
2. Change your organization's **Name**, or select **Upload logo** or **Change logo**.
3. Under **Email Addresses**, **Phone Numbers**, and **Addresses**, add the contact details residents should see. Select **Add Email** or **Add Address** to add one.

When you add an email address, we send a verification email to it. The address shows **Verified** once someone confirms it. To remove an email, phone number, or address, open its **More options** menu and select **Delete**. This can't be undone.

## Add a sub-organization

A sub-organization is a separate department inside your workspace, such as *Parks and Recreation*, with its own [contact book](/getting-started/glossary).

1. In **Settings**, select **General**.
2. Select **Create Sub-Organization**.
3. Enter the **Name**.
4. Enter a **Contact Book Name**.
5. Check the **Handle**, the short name used in links, such as *parks_and_rec*.
6. Save.

The new sub-organization appears in your workspace with its own contact book.
