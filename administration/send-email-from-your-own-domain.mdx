---
title: "Send email from your own domain"
---

Residents are more likely to open and trust an email that comes from an address they recognize, such as *notices@lakewoodco.gov*. To send Polimorphic email from your own domain, you'll add a few CNAME records at your DNS provider.

## Before you begin

- Set this up from the top-level organization in your workspace.
- Loop in someone who can change DNS (Domain Name System) records for your domain, usually your IT team or web host. Polimorphic can't make this change for you.
- CNAME stands for canonical name. It's a type of DNS record.

## Add your domain

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu.
2. Select **Email Domains**.
3. In **Email Domain**, enter your domain, such as *lakewoodco.gov*. Don't include the part before the @ sign.
4. Select **Add domain**.
5. Select **Set up verification**.

## Add the DNS records at your provider

1. Select **Show DNS records**.
2. For each record, select **Copy record name** and paste it into your DNS provider.
3. Select **Copy record value** and paste it into your DNS provider.
4. Set each record's type to CNAME, as shown in the **Type** column.
5. Save the records at your DNS provider.

## Check that your domain is verified

1. Return to **Email Domains**.
2. Select **Check verification status**.

Verification usually finishes within minutes but can take up to 72 hours while DNS changes spread. **Pending DNS Verification** during the first day is normal.

Once the domain shows **Verified**, staff can pick an address on that domain as the sender for [outbound communications](/getting-started/glossary). If you send from an address on a domain that isn't verified, the send fails.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not verified** | You added the domain but haven't started verification. |
| **Pending DNS Verification** | Polimorphic is waiting to see your DNS records. |
| **Verified** | You can send from this domain. |
| **Failed** | The records weren't found or don't match. Check them with your IT team. |
