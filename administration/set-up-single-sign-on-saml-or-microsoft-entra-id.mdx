---
title: "Set up single sign-on (SAML or Microsoft Entra ID)"
---

Staff already have a Microsoft work account, so there's no reason for them to juggle another password. With single sign-on ([SSO](/getting-started/glossary)), they sign in to Polimorphic with that account using SAML (Security Assertion Markup Language) 2.0, and your IT team stays in control of who gets in.

## Before you begin

- Loop in someone who can create an enterprise application in your Microsoft Entra tenant, usually your IT team.
- Pick which staff will be **Break-glass users**. These people can always sign in with a password, so you aren't locked out if SSO stops working. Pick at least one administrator.
- Know your staff email domain, such as *cityofmarion.gov*.

## Start the setup

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu.
2. Select **SSO & Identity**.
3. Under **Identity Providers**, find **Microsoft Entra** and select **Configure**.

The setup has three steps: **Identity Provider Setup**, **Polimorphic Configuration**, and **Test Connection**.

## Step 1: Give Microsoft the Polimorphic details

1. Copy **Identifier (Entity ID)** and paste it into your Entra enterprise application's SAML settings.
2. Copy **Reply URL (Assertion Consumer Service URL)** and paste it into Entra.
3. Copy **Sign-on URL** and paste it into Entra.
4. Copy **Log-out URL** and paste it into Entra.
5. In Entra, assign the staff or groups who should use Polimorphic.
6. Go to the next step in Polimorphic.

## Step 2: Give Polimorphic the Microsoft details

Your IT team finds these values in the Entra enterprise application.

1. Enter **Azure AD Tenant ID**.
2. Enter **Application (Client) ID**.
3. Enter **Login URL**.
4. Enter **Azure AD Identifier**.
5. Paste the Base64 version of the **SAML signing certificate**.
6. Go to the next step.

## Step 3: Test the connection

1. Select **Test Connection**.
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account that's assigned to the app.

If you see **Connection successful!**, you're ready for the next section. If you see **Connection test found issues**, check each copied value for extra spaces and try again.

## Pick how staff sign in

1. In **Email domains**, select the domains that should use SSO.
2. In **Break-glass users**, select the people who can always use a password.
3. In **Authentication mode**, pick one:
   - **Password Only**: SSO is off.
   - **Dual (Password + SSO)**: staff can use either. Use this while you test.
   - **SSO Only**: staff have to use SSO, except break-glass users. Add at least one break-glass user first.
4. Optional: set a **Session timeout** in hours or days.
5. Optional: in **Default group**, select the group new SSO users join.
6. Optional: under **Group mappings**, match each Entra group (by its **Group object ID**) to a Polimorphic group.
7. Select **Save**.

Staff with your email domain can now sign in with their Microsoft account. Groups that SSO manages show a lock icon in Access, and their members update each time someone signs in. Break-glass users can still sign in with a password.

**Tip:** Start in **Dual (Password + SSO)** mode and give staff a heads-up before you switch to **SSO Only**, so nobody is surprised at sign-in.
