---
title: "Create and edit groups and roles"
description: "Set up teams and the access they need in Settings."
---

When a new team starts using Polimorphic, or a team's job changes, you'll want their access to match. You do that with groups and roles: a group is a team of people, and a role is the set of permissions you give that group. If you're new to how these fit together, start with [Roles and permissions](/administration/roles-and-permissions).

## Create a group

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu, then select **Groups** under **Access**.
2. Select **Add Group**.
3. Enter a **Group Name**, such as *Code Enforcement*.
4. Optional: add people under **Group Members (optional)**.
5. Select **Save**.

The group shows in the list. It doesn't grant any access until you give it a role.

## Create a role

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu, then select **Roles** under **Access**.
2. Select **Add Role**.
3. Enter a **Role Name**, such as *Permit Clerk*.
4. Optional: under **Template**, pick a starting point, such as *Staff* or *Finance Admin*. You can adjust the permissions afterward.
5. Under **Group**, pick the group that should get this role.
6. Select **Save**.

Everyone in that group gets the role's permissions.

**Tip:** Starting from a template is usually faster than building a role from scratch. See [Roles and permissions matrix](/administration/roles-and-permissions-matrix) for what each permission allows.

## Share a role with other organizations

If your workspace has several organizations, you can turn on **Cross-Organization Role** when you create a role. Other organizations in the workspace can then assign it to their groups. Your organization keeps control of its name and permissions.

If you leave it off, only groups in your organization can be assigned the role.

## Add or remove people from a group

1. Open **Groups** under **Access**.
2. Find the group. You can use **Search groups...** to find it quickly.
3. To add someone, use **Add member...** and pick their name.
4. To take someone out, select **Remove member**.

Their access changes as soon as you save. Someone who's removed from their only group loses the access that group gave them, so give them a heads-up first.

To move someone to a different team instead, see [Invite staff and manage accounts](/administration/invite-staff-and-manage-accounts#move-someone-to-a-different-group).

## Delete a group or role

1. Open **Groups** or **Roles** under **Access**.
2. Select **Delete Group** or **Delete Role** next to it, and confirm.

People lose the access that group or role gave them. If you're deleting a group that still has members, you can pick **Reassign to another group** to move them first.

## If you can't change a group

- **Groups synced from single sign-on** show a lock icon. Their members come from your identity provider, so change membership there. See [Set up single sign-on](/administration/set-up-single-sign-on-saml-or-microsoft-entra-id).
- **A group that gives someone permission to manage users** can't be removed while it's needed for that access.
