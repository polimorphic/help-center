---
title: "I found a bug! How do I get it fixed?"
---

We test carefully, but sometimes a bug slips through. If something isn't working the way it should, let us know at [support@polimorphic.com](mailto:support@polimorphic.com). It helps to include what you were doing, what you expected, and a screenshot if you have one. We'll get it fixed as quickly as we can.
