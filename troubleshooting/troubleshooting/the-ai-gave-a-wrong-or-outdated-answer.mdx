---
title: "The AI gave a wrong or outdated answer"
---

When the AI gives a resident or staff member a wrong or outdated answer, the fix is almost always in the sources it read. Start by finding the cause below.

## A source is out of date

The AI repeats what it reads. If last year's fee schedule is still in the [Knowledge Base](/getting-started/glossary), residents get last year's fees.

**Fix:**

1. Open the interaction in the [interaction logs](/getting-started/glossary) and look at the sources it used.
2. Update or archive the old document or page.
3. For a website, refresh the crawl or set a regular cadence. See Crawl your website and set a refresh cadence.

The next answer uses the updated source.

## The right answer isn't in any source

**Fix:** Add an [FAQ](/getting-started/glossary) (frequently asked question) with the correct answer, or upload the document that has it. See Add documents, links, and FAQs.

## Two sources disagree

For example, a web page says the transfer station closes at 4 PM and a PDF says 5 PM.

**Fix:** Correct or archive the wrong source so only one answer is left.

## The source is set to Staff only

Residents don't get answers from sources set to **Staff only**.

**Fix:** If the information is public, change the source's **Availability** to **Everyone**.

## A connected system or map layer is out of date

**Fix:** Check the [GIS](/getting-started/glossary) layer or integration the answer used. Refresh the layer, or make sure the integration isn't marked **Inactive**.

## You don't see the tabs or buttons to fix it

Editing sources takes Knowledge Base edit access. Ask your administrator for the *Knowledge Base Manager* role template, or send them the details so they can make the change.

## When to contact support

If the sources are correct and the AI still gets it wrong, contact support. Include the date and time, the question, the answer it gave, and the correct answer.
