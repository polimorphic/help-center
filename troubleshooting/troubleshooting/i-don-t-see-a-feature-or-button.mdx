---
title: "I don't see a feature or button"
---

It's frustrating when a coworker can see a menu item, tab, or button and you can't. The cause is usually one of three things: your access, the organization you're working in, or a feature your organization doesn't have yet.

## Your role doesn't include it

What you can see depends on the groups and roles your administrator gives you. For example, staff who can only view settings see the **Integrations** tab but not the buttons to add one.

**Fix:**

1. Ask a coworker who has the button to confirm they're in the same organization.
2. Send your administrator the page name and the missing button.
3. Your administrator compares your groups and roles with your coworker's and adds the permission you need.

Once it's added, refresh the page and the item appears.

## You're in a different organization

If you work for more than one organization, such as a city and its utility, your access can differ in each one.

**Fix:** Check which organization is selected, and switch to the right one. The page reloads with your access for that organization.

## The item is read-only right now

Some buttons are grayed out on purpose because the item can't be changed right now. For example, live [workflows](/getting-started/glossary) can't be fully edited. See [Publish a new version of a workflow](/building-workflows/publish-a-new-version-of-a-workflow).

**Fix:** Hover over the grayed-out button to see why. To change a live workflow, duplicate it and edit the copy.

## Your organization doesn't have the feature

Polimorphic turns some features on for each organization. If no one in your organization sees it, this is the likely cause.

**Fix:** Contact Polimorphic support and describe what you're looking for. We'll let you know whether it's available for your organization.

## The page didn't finish loading

**Fix:** Refresh the page. If that doesn't help, sign out and sign back in so your latest permissions load.

## When to contact support

If your administrator confirms you have the right access and the item still doesn't show up, contact support. Include:

- Your email address and organization.
- The page you were on and the item you expected.
- The name of a coworker who can see it, if any.
