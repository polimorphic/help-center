---
title: "Add or edit a contact"
---

When you start working with a new resident, business, or group, add them to your [contact book](/getting-started/glossary) so their [cases](/getting-started/glossary), calls, and messages stay together. You can update their details any time.

Before you add someone, search first to make sure they aren't already there. Type at least three characters in **Search contacts...** on the [Contacts](/getting-started/glossary) page.

## Add a new contact

1. Select **Contacts** in the left sidebar.
2. Select **Add Contact**.
3. Pick **Individual** for a person or **Organization** for a business or group.
4. Enter the name. For a person, enter at least a first and last name. For an organization, enter the **Organization Name**.
5. Add an email address, phone number, and address if you have them.
6. Optional: add **Related Contacts** and **Tags**.
7. Select **Save Contact**.

The contact shows up in your contact book. Polimorphic uses their email address and phone number to link future cases and calls to them. You can also select **Add contact** from the search bar at the top of any page.

## Update a contact's details

1. Select **Contacts** in the left sidebar.
2. Search for the contact and select their name.
3. Select **Edit Contact**.
4. Change what you need to.
5. Select **Update Contact**.

The contact's page shows the new details right away.

## Tips

- Add an email address or phone number whenever you can. Without one, Polimorphic can't match future requests to this contact.
- If an email or phone number shows as invalid, check for typos. Polimorphic won't save an invalid value.
- Tags such as *Contractor* or *Landlord* make it easy to group contacts you message often.
