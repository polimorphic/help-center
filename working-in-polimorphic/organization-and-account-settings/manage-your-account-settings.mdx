---
title: "Manage your account settings"
description: "Update your name, email addresses, phone numbers, password, and email notifications."
---

When your name changes, you get a new work phone, or you're getting more email than you'd like, you can update it yourself in your account settings. These settings only affect your own account.

## Open your account settings

1. Press **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on a Mac) to open the search bar at the top of the page.
2. Type *Account* and select **Account Settings**.

You can also open **Account Settings** from the menu at the top of the page. Use the menu on the left to move between sections.

## Update your name

1. Select **Profile**.
2. Select **Edit Personal Details**.
3. Change your **First Name** or **Last Name**, then save.

## Add or change an email address

1. Select **Emails**.
2. Add the new address.
3. Open the verification email we send to that address and confirm it. Until you do, the address shows **Pending verification**.
4. To use it as your main address, select **Set as Primary email**.

You can also edit your email signature from this page.

## Add a phone number or address

1. Select **Phones** or **Addresses**.
2. Add the number or address, then save.

## Change your password

1. Select **Password**.
2. Enter your **Current Password**, then your **New Password**, and enter it again in **Confirm Password**.
3. Save.

Use your new password the next time you log in. If you don't remember your current one, see [Log in and reset your password](/getting-started/log-in-and-reset-your-password).

If your organization uses single sign-on, you sign in with your work account instead, and your password is managed there.

## Choose your email notifications

1. Select **Notifications**.
2. Turn **Enable Email Notifications** on or off.
3. Pick how often you get a summary email: **Daily Digest**, **Weekly Digest**, or **Do not send Digest**.
4. Optional: select **Send Test Digest Email** to see what the summary looks like.

You'll see *Digest email sent successfully* when the test is on its way.
