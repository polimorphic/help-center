---
title: "Case statuses and the document center"
---

A [case](/getting-started/glossary)'s status tells everyone where the request stands, and the **Documents** tab keeps every file for it in one place. Use this page when you're not sure which status to pick or where a file went.

## Case statuses

| Status | What it means | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unsubmitted** | A resident started the request but hasn't finished or submitted it yet. | Hidden from most case views. Filter by this status to find residents who may be stuck on their application. |
| **Open** | Staff are working the case. All step assignments are active. | Shows in most case views, including **My To-Do**. |
| **Closed** | The work is finished, or the request was denied. Step assignments stop. | Leaves active views such as **My To-Do**, but still counts in reports for the [workflow](/getting-started/glossary). |
| **Archived** | The case was created by mistake or as a test. | Hidden from most case views and left out of workflow reports. The case is kept and is read-only. |
| **Hidden** | The case is kept out of everyday work. | Hidden from most case views and left out of workflow reports. You can't choose this status from a case. |

### Change a case's status

- A workflow step can change the status for you, such as from **Unsubmitted** to **Open** when staff review begins.
- Pick a new status from the status selector at the top of a case.
- To close several cases at once, select them in the Cases table and choose **Close cases**.

The new status shows at the top of the case, and the case moves in or out of views like **My To-Do** to match.

### What you can do in each status

- **Archived** cases are read-only.
- **Closed** cases may offer fewer actions, depending on your organization's settings.

## Find a case's files on the Documents tab

The **Documents** tab on a case collects every file connected to it. Filter chips at the top sort files into three groups.

| Group | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Submission** | Files residents or staff uploaded in a workflow form, such as a site plan or proof of insurance. |
| **Generated** | Documents Polimorphic created from a template in the workflow, such as a permit certificate or approval letter. |
| **Other** | Files attached to notes or emails, and files staff uploaded on this tab. Downloads and exports label this group "Uploaded." |

### Actions on the Documents tab

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Upload files** | Adds files to the case. They appear under **Other**. |
| **Generate document** | Creates a document from the workflow's template. It stays grayed out until the required fields are filled in. |
| **Select a document** | Opens the file in a new browser tab. Each card shows who added it and when. |
