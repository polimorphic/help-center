---
title: "Communicate with a resident from a case"
---

When you're working a [case](/getting-started/glossary), you'll often need to reach the resident, or record a call or visit so your coworkers know what was said. Doing it from the case keeps the whole conversation in one place for everyone on your team.

## Before you begin

- Your organization's sending email domain needs to be set up so emails can go out. See [Send email from your own domain](/administration/send-email-from-your-own-domain).
- The resident needs an email address on the case or on their [contact](/getting-started/glossary) record.

## Send an email from a case

1. Open the case.
2. Select **Send email** at the top of the case.
3. In **From**, pick the address to send from.
4. Check the **To** field. Polimorphic fills in the [case requester](/getting-started/glossary). Add **Cc** or **Bcc** recipients if you need to.
5. Check the **Subject**. Polimorphic fills in the case name.
6. Optional: select **Apply Form Letter** to start from a saved template.
7. Write your message.
8. Optional: select **Attach Files** to add documents.
9. Select **Send**.

Your email shows on the case's **Activity** tab under **Emails**. When the resident replies, the reply shows up there too.

## Add an email from your own inbox

If a resident emailed you directly, you can still get that conversation onto the case. Every case has its own email address, and anything sent to it shows up on the case.

1. On the case, select the **Activity** tab.
2. Select **Leave a note or add attachment**.
3. Select **Add Email**.
4. Copy the case email address.
5. Forward the related email from your inbox to that address, or add it in Cc on future emails.

The forwarded email appears on the case's **Activity** tab.

## Log a call or visit

1. On the case, select the **Activity** tab.
2. Select **Leave a note or add attachment**.
3. Type what was discussed, such as *Called resident. Inspection moved to Thursday 10 a.m.*
4. Optional: select **Attach Files**.
5. Select **Add Note**.

Your note shows under **Notes** on the **Activity** tab, where anyone who opens the case can read it. Notes are internal, so residents never see them. To loop in a coworker, type *@* and their name in the note, and they'll get an email with a link to the case.

## Good to know

- When a [workflow](/getting-started/glossary) step sends a message that needs staff review, it shows up as a **Send Communication** task. Open the task to check or edit the message before it goes out.
- If emails aren't going out, the sending domain is the first thing to check with your administrator.
