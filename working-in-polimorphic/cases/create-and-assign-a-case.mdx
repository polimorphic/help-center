---
title: "Create and assign a case"
---

When a resident calls, emails, or walks in with a request, you can open a [case](/getting-started/glossary) for them by hand and assign it to the right person, so the work has an owner from the start.

## Before you begin

- Know who the case is for. If they're already a [contact](/getting-started/glossary), you can select them. If not, you can add them later.
- Optional: know which [workflow](/getting-started/glossary) fits the request, such as "Building Permit Renewal." For general questions, you can open a case without one.

## Open a case for a resident

1. Select the search bar at the top of any page.
2. Select **Open manual case**.
3. Optional: in **Workflow**, search for and pick the workflow this case should follow.
4. Optional: in **Case Requester**, search for and pick the resident.
5. In **Case Subject**, enter a name you'll recognize later, such as *Permit renewal, 48 Maple Ave*. This is the only required field.
6. Under **Log Interaction**, pick how the resident reached you: **Note**, **Email**, **Call**, or **In-Person Visit**.
7. Type what the resident told you.
8. Optional: add a **Case Location** and **Tags**.
9. Select **Start Case**.

The case opens and shows up in the Cases list. You can also open a case from a contact's page by selecting **Open Case**.

## Pick who owns the case

The [case manager](/getting-started/glossary) is the staff member responsible for the case overall.

1. On the case, select the **Case Manager** card.
2. Search for the staff member and select their name.

Their name shows on the **Case Manager** card.

## Assign a step or task to someone

1. In the case's tasks area, select **All**.
2. Open the menu on the step or task and select **Manage Assignees**.
3. In **Select Assignee**, search for and pick the staff member.
4. Pick **Assign and Notify** to make them responsible and email them, or **Notify Only** to email them without making them responsible.
5. Save your changes.

Assigned staff see the case on their **My To-Do** tab with a **Your Turn** label, and anyone you notified gets an email. The assignment also shows on the case's **Activity** tab.

## Add a one-off task

1. In the case's tasks area, select **Create task**.
2. Enter a **Task Name**, such as *Call contractor to confirm inspection date*.
3. In **Assignees**, pick one or more staff members.
4. Optional: set a **Due Date** and add a **Description**.
5. Select **Create**.

The task appears in the case's tasks area and on each [assignee](/getting-started/glossary)'s **My To-Do** tab.

## Good to know

- Due dates can't be in the past.
- When a case follows a workflow, the workflow may assign steps for you. Change those only when the default owner isn't right for this case.
