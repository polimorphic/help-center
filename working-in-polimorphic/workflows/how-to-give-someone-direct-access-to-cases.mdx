---
title: "How to give someone direct access to cases"
---

Sometimes you need to bring someone into a case who isn't on your team, like a contractor, an inspector from another department, or a partner agency. Direct access lets you share cases with that one person without adding them to a group.

You can share every case in a workflow, a single step, or a single case.

## Share all the cases in a workflow

1. Go to **Workflows** in the left-hand sidebar and open the workflow.
2. Click the **Manage access to cases** icon near the top of the page.
3. Enter the person's email address, pick whether they can view or edit, and decide if you'd like to send them an email notification.
4. Save.

They can now open the cases in this workflow at the access level you picked.

## Share a single step

1. Open the workflow in the editor and select the block you'd like to share.
2. In the sidebar, select **Manage access**.
3. Add the person, pick their access level, and save.

They now have access to that step.

## Share a single case

1. Open the case in the Case Feed.
2. Select **Manage access** at the top right of the case, then **Share with staff member**.
3. Add the person, pick their access level, and save.

They can now open this case at the access level you picked.

To invite someone as a case member instead, select **Manage access**, then **Invite case member**. This is separate from granting staff direct access.

## Remove someone's access

Open the same access dialog and remove the person from the list. For a single case, select **Manage access**, then **Share with staff member** to return to it. Their direct access ends immediately.

**Tip:** If they're still working on something, give them a quick heads-up before you remove their access.
