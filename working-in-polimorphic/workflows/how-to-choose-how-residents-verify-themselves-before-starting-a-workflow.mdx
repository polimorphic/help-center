---
title: "How to choose how residents verify themselves before starting a workflow"
---

Some requests need to know exactly who's applying, while others, like a pothole report, work best when anyone can submit in seconds. For external workflows, you can pick what a resident has to provide before they start a case.

## Pick how residents verify themselves

1. Select **Workflows** in the left menu and open the workflow.
2. Select **Workflow Settings** in the editor sidebar.
3. Under **Access to Start Workflow**, make sure **External** is selected. **Applicant Verification** only appears for external workflows.
4. Under **Applicant Verification**, pick one of the options below.

## The options

- **Account sign-in required**: residents sign in to an account before they apply.
- **Email address required**: residents enter an email address to apply. The address is collected but not verified.
- **No account or email required**: residents can apply without signing in or giving an email address.

Your choice saves as soon as you pick it, and new applicants see the matching step when they start. If it can't be saved, the previous option comes back and an error message appears. Try again, or contact support if it keeps happening.

## Which option to pick

- Use **Account sign-in required** when residents need to come back to track the case, pay, or see private information.
- Use **Email address required** when you need a way to reach the resident but don't want to ask them to create an account.
- Use **No account or email required** for anonymous reports, such as a pothole or graffiti report.
