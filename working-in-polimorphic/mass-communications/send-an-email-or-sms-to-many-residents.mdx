---
title: "Send an email or SMS to many residents"
---

When a water main breaks or a road closes, you need to reach a lot of residents quickly. [Outbound communications](/getting-started/glossary) let you send one email or text message to many residents at once, such as a boil-water notice for one neighborhood.

## Before you begin

- Your organization needs email or SMS (text messaging) turned on. If neither is on, the create screen tells you to contact support.
- To send texts, your organization needs a phone line set up for texting.
- To send email from your own domain, the domain needs to be verified. See [Send email from your own domain](/administration/send-email-from-your-own-domain).
- For **All Contacts** or **Custom** recipients, residents need to be in your [contact book](/getting-started/glossary) with an email address or phone number. A **One-time list** can include people who aren't in the contact book.

## Create the communication

1. Select the search bar at the top of any page.
2. Select **Outbound Communications**.
3. Select **Create Outbound Communication**.
4. In **Name**, enter a name staff will recognize, such as *Boil water notice, Oak Hill*. Residents don't see this name.
5. Under **Channel**, select **Email** or **SMS**.
6. Select **Create and Configure**.

The new communication opens so you can set it up.

## Pick who it's from

1. Under **From**, check the sender. For email, select the sender address. For texts, the phone line is filled in for you.

## Pick who gets it

1. Under recipients, select **All Contacts** to send to everyone, or **Custom** to narrow the list.
2. If you picked **Custom**, the recipient setup opens on the **Send To** step.
3. Optional: in **Tags**, select tags. [Contacts](/getting-started/glossary) with these tags are included.
4. Optional: in **Case Requestors for Workflows**, select [workflows](/getting-started/glossary). People who submitted requests in those workflows are included.
5. Optional: pick an area. Select **Draw Polygon** to draw a shape on the map, **Circle Radius** to pick a point and a **Radius**, or **GIS Layer** to use a map layer, such as a council district. [GIS](/getting-started/glossary) stands for geographic information system.
6. Go to the **Do Not Send To** step.
7. Optional: in **Exclude by Tags**, select tags to leave out, such as *Do not contact*.
8. Optional: search for specific people and remove them.
9. Go to the **Review** step and check the list.
10. Save the recipients.

Each contact gets one message, even if they have several email addresses or phone numbers. People who texted STOP to your phone line are left out of texts.

### Use a one-time list

1. Under recipients, select **One-time list**.
2. Select **Upload CSV**, then **Choose CSV file**, or select **Paste** to enter one person per line and **Use pasted list**.
3. For a CSV, include a header row with an **Email** column for email or a **Phone** column for SMS. Optional **Name**, **First Name**, and **Last Name** columns help personalize the message.
4. Review the recipients and uncheck anyone you'd like to leave out. Invalid addresses or numbers, duplicate destinations, and people who opted out of texts are skipped.
5. Optional: select **Also add people who are not in the contact book yet**. New contacts are added when the message is sent; existing contacts aren't changed.
6. Select **Use [count] recipient(s)** to save the list.

The list stays with this communication and doesn't tag anyone.

## Write the message

1. For email, enter a subject in **Add a subject line**. A subject is required.
2. In **Body**, write your message.
3. Optional: insert **First Name** or **Full Name** to personalize each message.
4. Select **Send Test**. The test goes to your own email address or phone number.
5. Read the test and fix anything that looks wrong.

**Tip:** Ask a coworker to read the test too. A second set of eyes catches typos and wrong dates before thousands of residents see them.

## Send it now or schedule it

1. Select **Review & Send Email** or **Review & Send SMS**.
2. Select **Send now**, or select **Schedule for later** and pick a date and time.
3. Select **Confirm Send** or **Schedule**.

To change a scheduled send, open it and select **Reschedule**, or cancel the schedule.

The communication goes out in batches. On the **Outbound Communications** list, you can open it to see results, such as how many were **Delivered**, **Not delivered**, or **Pending**, and the **Response Rate**. Replies from residents are logged as responses.
