A lot of resident requests still arrive in someone's inbox. With **Polimorphic for Outlook**, you can connect those emails to cases, reuse your team's templates, and draft a reply, all from the email you're reading.

## Before you begin

- You need a Polimorphic account.
- The add-in has to be installed in your Outlook. If you don't see it, email support@polimorphic.com and we'll help you get it set up.

## Open Polimorphic in Outlook

1. Open an email in Outlook.
2. Open the **Polimorphic for Outlook** add-in from the email's toolbar.
3. If you work in more than one organization, pick one under **Select an organization**.

The add-in has three tabs: **CRM**, **Templates**, and **Assistant**.

## Link an email to a case

1. Select the **CRM** tab.
2. Polimorphic checks for a matching case. If it finds one, you'll see **Case Found** with the requester and who it's assigned to.
3. Select **Import Email** to save the email to Polimorphic.

You'll see **Email Logged** once it's saved, and the email shows up in Polimorphic so your team can see it. If there's no matching case, you can create one from the same tab.

## Use a template

1. Select the **Templates** tab.
2. Search for a template with **Search templates...** and pick one.
3. Paste it into your reply.

The template is copied for you when you select it.

## Draft a reply with AI

1. Select the **Assistant** tab.
2. Select **Draft Reply with AI**.

Polimorphic writes a reply from the email and your organization's information and opens it as a draft reply in Outlook. Read it over and make any changes before you send it.
