---
title: "How replies to case emails reach your team"
---

When you email a resident from a case, their reply comes back to the case automatically, so the whole conversation stays in one place. There's no reply-to address to set up in account settings anymore.

## How it works

- Every case email goes out with a reply address that belongs to that case.
- When a resident replies, the message is added to the case activity timeline.
- Residents see the case reply address, not a staff member's personal email.

## Let your team know when a reply arrives

1. Go to **Workflows** in the left-hand sidebar and open the workflow.
2. Open workflow settings.
3. Turn on the notification for inbound email sent to the case address.
4. Save and publish the workflow.

From then on, staff assigned to open steps get an email when a reply comes in. If nobody is assigned, the case manager gets it. This setting is on by default, so turn it off for any workflow where staff shouldn't be notified.
