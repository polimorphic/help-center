---
title: "Capturing Replies to Emails"
---

When you email a resident from a case, you'll want their reply to land in the same place. Polimorphic does that for you: replies are added to the case automatically.

## How it works

- **Each case has its own reply address.** Every email sent from a case uses a reply address that belongs to that case.
- **Replies show up on the case.** When a resident replies, the message is added to the case activity timeline.
- **Residents see the case address.** They see the case reply address, not a staff member's personal email.
- **Your team hears about it.** Staff assigned to open steps get an email when a reply arrives. If nobody is assigned, the case manager gets it instead. This is on by default, and you can turn it off in workflow settings.
