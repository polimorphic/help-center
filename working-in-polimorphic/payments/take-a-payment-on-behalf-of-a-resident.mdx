---
title: "Take a payment on behalf of a resident"
---

Not every resident pays online. When someone calls in or stops by the front counter to pay a renewal fee, you can take the payment for them in Polimorphic so it's recorded alongside everything else.

## Before you begin

- The bill needs to exist already. Bills are created by a [workflow](/getting-started/glossary) payment step or added to a payment type.
- Have something that identifies the bill, such as the account number, bill name, or bill description.
- To take a card in person, your office needs a card reader that Polimorphic has set up.

## Start a payment

You can start from the Payments page or from a [case](/getting-started/glossary).

- **From Payments:** select **Payments** in the left menu, open the payment type on the **Payment Types** tab, and select **Take Payment**. The payment screen opens in a new tab.
- **From a case:** find the payment task in the case's tasks area and select **Take Payment**.

The payment screen has three stages: **Find Payment**, **Select Payer**, and **Process Payment**. If you start from a case, the bill is already selected.

## Find the bill

1. In **Select Payment Type**, select the type of bill, such as *Water Utility*.
2. Enter what you know in **Search by ID Number**, **Search by Bill Name**, or **Search by Bill Description**.
3. Select **Find Payment**.
4. Under **Search Results**, select the bill that matches.
5. Select **Next**.

If the wrong bills come back, select **Search Again** and try a different value.

## Pick who's paying

1. Search your [contacts](/getting-started/glossary) for the person paying.
2. Select their name. If they aren't a contact yet, select **Add contact** and enter their details.
3. Select **Next**.

## Collect the payment

Check the charges and the total with the resident before you collect anything. Then pick one option.

### Take a card payment

1. Select **Take Card Payment**.
2. Pick **Credit Card** or **ACH / E-Check**.
3. Enter the card or bank details the resident gives you.
4. Submit the payment.

If your office has a card reader, pick **Reader** instead, select the reader, and select **Send Payment to Reader**. Ask the resident to tap or insert their card. The screen shows **Waiting for Payment** until the reader responds.

### Record a payment you already received

Use this for cash, a check, a money order, or a card or bank payment made outside Polimorphic.

1. Select **Record Payment**.
2. Pick **Cash**, **Check**, **Money Order**, **Credit Card**, or **eCheck / ACH**.
3. Confirm the payment.

### Waive the bill

1. Select **Waive**.

The bill shows as **Waived**. If you waive a bill by mistake, select **Unwaive**.

### Send the bill to the resident

1. Select **Send Bill**.
2. In **Email payment link**, enter the resident's email address.
3. Select **Send**.

The resident gets a link to pay online. The link works for three days.

## What happens after a payment

A completed payment shows a confirmation and a receipt. The payment appears on the **Transactions** tab. If the bill belongs to a case, the payment, waiver, or recorded payment also appears in the case's activity, with the amount and who did it.

A bank (ACH) payment entered by hand can show as processing until the bank account is verified.
