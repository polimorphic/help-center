---
title: "How to connect your Stripe account to accept payments"
---

If residents are still mailing checks or lining up at the counter to pay, online payments can save everyone a trip. Polimorphic uses Stripe to collect card and ACH (bank) payments on cases and bills, so the first step is connecting a Stripe account. New Stripe accounts are set up as government entities, and Stripe asks for a few items during verification.

## Get payments turned on

Polimorphic sets up payments with you. To get started, contact your Customer Success Manager or [support@polimorphic.com](mailto:support@polimorphic.com) to turn on payments for your organization. They can walk you through the steps below, help with the authorization letter, and set up payment steps in your workflows.

## What you'll need

- **An account representative.** One individual who will manage the Stripe account.
- **A signed authorization letter on official letterhead.** Stripe requires this to activate the account. It must be signed by a senior official of the entity (for example the mayor, city or county manager, department head, treasurer, attorney, or council chair) who is not the account representative.
- **The details that go in the letter.** The entity's legal name exactly as it appears on the Stripe account, the date, the entity's address, your website URL, your tax ID (EIN), the Stripe account ID, the account representative's business email, and a statement authorizing them to manage the account.
- **A bank account for payouts.** The routing and account numbers of an account held in the entity's name.

## Connect your Stripe account

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu and open the **Payments** tab.
2. Select **Connect with Stripe**.
3. Review the list of items Stripe will ask for, then continue to Stripe.
4. Complete the Stripe-hosted onboarding form. When you finish, you're returned to Polimorphic.
5. Once your authorization letter is ready, contact your Customer Success Manager to submit it to Stripe.

The **Payments** tab shows that your Stripe account is connected.

## Pick how payouts reach your bank

Under **How should payouts be sent?** on the Payments tab, pick one option:

- **Automatic payouts**: each inbound payment is paid out to your bank account as it's paid.
- **Manual payouts**: funds stay in your Stripe balance until you start a payout.

Use **Open Stripe dashboard** to view transactions and balances in Stripe.

## Decide who covers processing fees

Under **Who covers the processing fee?**, pick one option:

- **Payer covers fee**: the processing fee is added to the amount the payer is charged.
- **Organization covers fee**: your organization absorbs the fee, and it's taken out of your payouts.

Your choices save on the **Payments** tab and apply to new payments.
