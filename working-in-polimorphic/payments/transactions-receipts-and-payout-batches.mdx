---
title: "Transactions, receipts, and payout batches"
---

When you're reconciling at the end of the day or tracking down a resident's payment, you'll be on the **Transactions** and **Payout Batches** tabs. Here's what every column, filter, and status means, plus what's on a receipt.

To find these tabs, select **Payments** in the left menu. The Payments page has three tabs: **Payment Types**, **Transactions**, and **Payout Batches**.

## Transactions tab

A transaction is one payment attempt by one payer.

### Columns

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Attempted On** | When the payment started. |
| **Completed On** | When the payment finished. Blank if it hasn't finished. |
| **ID** | The payment's reference number. |
| **Payment Type** | The kind of bill, such as *Building Permit Fee*. |
| **Payer Name** | The person or business that paid. |
| **Case ID** | The [case](/getting-started/glossary) the bill belongs to, if any. |
| **Method** | How the payer paid. |
| **Amount** | The amount paid. |
| **Receipt** | Opens the receipt. |
| **Reconciled** | Whether your finance team has matched this payment to its records. |

### Filters

| Filter | Values |
| --- | --- |
| **Payment Method** | **Check**, **Cash**, **Credit Card**, **eCheck / ACH** |
| **Payment Type** | Your organization's payment types |
| **Reconciliation Status** | **Reconciled**, **Unreconciled** |
| **Payment Status** | **Processing**, **Succeeded**, **Failed** |
| **Date** | A date range |

### Payment status values

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Processing** | The payment started but hasn't finished. Bank (ACH) payments can stay here for a few days. |
| **Succeeded** | The payment finished. |
| **Failed** | The payment didn't go through. The resident still owes the amount. |

A bill's status is separate from a payment's status. A bill can show **Unpaid**, **Paid**, or **Waived** no matter how many payment attempts it had.

## Build a report or reconcile in bulk

**Batch Action** builds a transaction report and can mark payments as reconciled. It has five steps: **Identity**, **Payments**, **Payment Type**, **Review**, and **Download**.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Select Time Frame** | **All Time**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, or **Custom Range**. |
| **Paid through Polimorphic** | Includes payments made in Polimorphic. |
| **Payment Methods** | Limits the report to the methods you pick. |
| **Reconciliation Status** | **Unreconciled**, **Reconciled**, or **All**. |
| **Only select payments that are not yet in a payout batch** | Leaves out payments already grouped in a batch. |
| **Mark transactions downloaded in report as reconciled** | Marks every payment in the report as reconciled when you download it. |
| **Generate payout from selected transactions** | Groups the selected payments into a payout batch. |
| **Split each transaction into a separate batch** | Makes one batch per payment instead of one batch for all. |
| **Stripe Payout Descriptor (22 character limit)** | The short label that appears on your bank statement for the payout. |

## Track money going to your bank

A payout batch is a group of payments sent to your organization's bank account together. Card and bank payments go through Stripe, the payment processor.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount** | The batch total. |
| **Status** | How far along the payout is. |
| **Date** | When the batch was created. |
| **Stripe ID** | Stripe's reference for the payout. |
| **Descriptor** | The bank-statement label. |

You can filter by **Date** (**Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**) and by **Payment Type**, or use **Search payouts**.

Select a batch to open **Payout Details**. It lists each payment in the batch with its **Payer Name**, **Method**, and **Date**. The list loads when you open it. If it doesn't load, close it and open it again.

Two coworkers can see different transactions and payout batches, depending on their access to payments and payouts.

## What's on a receipt

A receipt opens with the heading **Payment Confirmation**. It shows:

- That the bill was paid, and when.
- Each charge and the total.
- Any processing fee, if the payer covered it.

Residents see the same receipt after they pay online.
