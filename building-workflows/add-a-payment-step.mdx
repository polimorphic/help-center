---
title: "Add a payment step"
---

Most permits and licenses come with a fee. A **Collect Payment** step bills the resident at the right point in a [workflow](/getting-started/glossary), and the [case](/getting-started/glossary) waits there until the bill is paid.

## Before you begin

- Your organization needs a connected Stripe account. If it isn't connected yet, the **Publish Workflow** window warns you. See [How to connect your Stripe account to accept payments](/working-in-polimorphic/payments/how-to-connect-your-stripe-account-to-accept-payments), or contact your Customer Success Manager to get payments set up.
- The workflow needs to be a draft.
- Have each fee name and amount ready, such as a $75 application fee.

## Add a fee to a workflow

1. Open the workflow and select **Edit Workflow**.
2. In **Add Steps**, select **Collect Payment**.
3. Under **Line items**, enter the fee name in **Item name**, such as *Application fee*.
4. Enter the fee in **Amount**, such as *75*.
5. To add another fee, select **Add** and fill in the next line.
6. To calculate a fee from the applicant's answers, select **Edit formula** on that line.
7. Check the **Total**.
8. Connect the payment step after the form that collects what the fee depends on.
9. If a staff member should hear when the bill is created, select **Add assignees**.

Once the workflow is published, each case that reaches this step gets a bill with your line items. The resident can pay online, or staff can take the payment for them. Steps after the payment step wait until the bill is paid.

**Tip:** Before you publish, run a test case through the workflow to check that the total looks right.
