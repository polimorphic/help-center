---
title: "Publish a new version of a workflow"
---

Fees change, forms need a new question, a review step moves to a different team. When a [workflow](/getting-started/glossary) is already live, you can make those changes in a new version without disrupting the [cases](/getting-started/glossary) already moving through it.

## Before you begin

- Know what happens to cases already in progress: they stay on the workflow they started on. They don't move to your new version.
- Pick a quiet time to switch, such as after business hours.

## Why you can't edit a live workflow

When a workflow is live, the editor shows "Edits to this workflow are limited because it is live or archived." This protects cases that are partway through. Changing the steps under them could leave them stuck.

## Make and publish the new version

1. Open the live workflow from **Workflows Library**.
2. Select the copy icon. Its tooltip reads **Duplicate workflow**.
3. On the **Duplicate Workflow** screen, give the copy a clear name, such as *Sidewalk dining renewal 2027*.
4. Select **Duplicate**.
5. Make your changes in the **Workflow Editor**.
6. Create a test case to check the changes.
7. Select **Publish**.
8. Select **Proceed to Publish**.

The new version is live and ready for new cases.

## Retire the old version

1. Update your website links and embeds to use the new workflow. Use **Share or Embed** in the editor to copy them.
2. Open the old workflow.
3. Open **Workflow Settings**.
4. Select **Archive**.

The old workflow stops taking new cases. Its existing cases stay where they are.

**Tip:** Let your team know before you switch versions, so nobody is surprised by a new form or step.

## What happens to cases in progress

- Cases that started on the old workflow finish on the old workflow, with its steps and fees.
- New cases use the new workflow.
- Archiving the old workflow stops new cases on it. Past and in-progress cases stay available.

New cases start on the latest published version, whether they come from the resident portal, an AI phone line, an integration, or staff. Staff keep working older cases where they are.
